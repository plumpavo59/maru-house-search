How scoring works

Each known rating is 0–10. Category ratings and Ru’s and Mara’s personal ratings all use their weights in Preferences.

Fit = 10 × Σ(rating × weight) ÷ Σ(assessed weights)
Assessed = assessed weights ÷ all weights
Overall = fit × (0.5 + 0.5 × assessed)
AssessedMaximum overall
10%55 / 100
50%75 / 100
100%100 / 100

Unknown isn’t zero. Zero is a known rating. No assessments means no score. Personal ratings are subjective inputs, not extra points added afterwards.
